How Can God Forgive and Still Be Good?

Romans 3:21–31 · August 2026
Big IdeaSince all have sinned, all are justified by faith in Jesus Christ, whom God presented as an atoning sacrifice to show his righteousness.

Aim

(1) Understand why grace is not God being soft; (2) stop building identity on performance or approval; (3) name one friend to pray for and invite.

You’ll need: Bibles (CSB), pens, about 60–75 minutes.

Connect (10 min)

  • Someone gets called out in a group chat. What does the chat actually want — an apology, punishment, or just the drama? When is calling out right, and when does it turn cruel?
  • If your feed is a highlight reel, what is the version of you that never gets posted? What would it cost to let someone see it?

Read — Romans 3:21-31 (CSB)

21 But now, apart from the law, the righteousness of God has been revealed, attested by the Law and the Prophets. 22 The righteousness of God is through faith in Jesus Christ to all who believe, since there is no distinction. 23 For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God; 24 they are justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.

25 God presented him as an atoning sacrifice in his blood, received through faith, to demonstrate his righteousness, because in his restraint God passed over the sins previously committed. 26 God presented him to demonstrate his righteousness at the present time, so that he would be just and justify the one who has faith in Jesus.

27 Where, then, is boasting? It is excluded. By what kind of law? By one of works? No, on the contrary, by a law of faith. 28 For we conclude that a person is justified by faith apart from the works of the law. 29 Or is God the God of Jews only? Is he not the God of Gentiles too? Yes, of Gentiles too, 30 since there is one God who will justify the circumcised by faith and the uncircumcised through faith. 31 Do we then nullify the law through faith? Absolutely not! On the contrary, we uphold the law.

Context

Romans 1-3 has been building a case: nobody is innocent — not the outsider, not the religious insider. Then verse 21 turns the corner with “But now”. God has revealed a righteousness that is not earned but received by trusting Jesus. Two big words: redemption (a price paid to buy someone out) and atoning sacrifice (Jesus absorbing the judgement our sin deserved). Grace is not God shrugging. It is God paying.

Discuss

  1. Read verses 21-22. What has actually changed at “But now”? What has not?
  2. Verse 23: no distinction, all have sinned. Our culture ranks people constantly — who is problematic, who is sorted. How does this verse dismantle the ranking?
  3. Verse 24 says justified freely by grace. If someone said “that’s too easy, people can just do whatever they want”, how would you answer from verses 25-26?
  4. David mentioned Alice Cooper — a preacher’s son who walked away, wrecked himself, and came back. Is there anyone you secretly think is too far gone for God? What does verse 23 say about that instinct?
  5. Where do you go looking for the verdict “you are enough” — marks, job title, follower count, a relationship? What happens when that verdict gets withdrawn?
  6. Verses 27-28 exclude boasting; faith receives with empty hands. What are you still trying to hold in your hands when you come to God?
  7. Verses 29-31: God justifies Jew and Gentile the same way. Who do you find hardest to imagine sitting next to in church, and why?
  8. Concretely, what would change on Monday — at uni, at work, in your share house — if your worth was already settled by Jesus and could not go up or down?

Leader’s Notes

Point joyfully to Jesus. Assume there are guests and not-yet-Christians in the room — never put anyone on the spot about their sin or their faith. Go first with your own honesty and let the passage do the work.

Brief answers to key questions

  • Q1: The verdict of guilt has not changed. What has changed is that God has revealed a righteousness apart from law-keeping, attested by the Law and the Prophets — it is not a loophole, it is the plan.
  • Q3: Grace is free to us and astronomically costly to God. Verses 25-26 exist precisely to answer the “too easy” objection: God had to be just, so he presented Jesus as the atoning sacrifice.
  • Q6: Faith is trust and treasure — open hands receiving a gift. Anything we clutch (achievement, reputation, control) is a hand not free to receive.
